Hi! I am Sydney Pugh, a postdoctoral researcher in the [Artificial Intelligence for Ambulatory Care Innovation (AI-4-AI) Lab](https://www.med.upenn.edu/kbjohnsonlab/) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/), where I am advised by [Kevin Johnson](https://www.kevinbjohnsonmd.net/). I am also a [Penn AI Fellow](https://web.sas.upenn.edu/data-science/postdoctoral-fellows/) and a [PennAITech Innovation Fellow](https://www.pennaitech.org/innovationfellows). My research focuses on developing trustworthy, interpretable, and user-centered AI tools that can move from development into real clinical use. I am particularly interested in methods that hold up under real-world data constraints, such as small datasets and limited ground truth, and in designing AI systems around the needs of the clinicians and patients who use them.

Currently, I lead the development of [WATCH-SS](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789819824755_0024), an explainable AI tool that screens for cognitive impairment from patient speech. My work on WATCH-SS spans model development and is now moving toward clinical validation and integration into primary care workflows. I also contribute to projects on chatbots for primary care and ethical oversight of clinical multi-agent AI systems.

I earned my Ph.D. in [Computer and Information Science (CIS)](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/) at the University of Pennsylvania in August 2024, where I was advised by [Insup Lee](https://www.cis.upenn.edu/~lee/home/index.shtml) and [James Weimer](https://jamesweimer.net/). My [dissertation](https://www.proquest.com/openview/d1142238baa72023e1668a6caf01e8a7/1?pq-origsite=gscholar&cbl=18750&diss=y) explored weakly-supervised methods for rapid and cost-effective performance evaluation of medical AI systems. I also earned my M.S.E. in CIS at UPenn in 2021. Prior to that, I earned my B.S. in Applied Mathematics and Computer Science from [Loyola University Maryland](https://www.loyola.edu/) in 2019.
